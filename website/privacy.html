Privacy policy

Bid2Scale is an independent business-preparation app for Indian small businesses selling on the Government e-Marketplace (GeM). It is not affiliated with GeM or the Government of India. This policy covers the Bid2Scale Android, iOS and web apps and this website.

In short: we use your information only to run Bid2Scale for you. We do not sell it. We never ask for your GeM password, OTPs, PAN or Aadhaar numbers. You can delete your account and data at any time.

Information we collect

  • Account: your email address, used for sign-in codes and email alerts.
  • Business profile: answers you give during onboarding — your name, business name and form, sector, what you supply, state and district, your role, turnover range, past order experience, and yes/no answers about which documents are ready. You choose what to enter.
  • Calculator results and progress: eligibility, revenue, pricing and ROI inputs and results, and your readiness checklist.
  • Bid alert settings: the keywords and states you chose, your channel choices (app, email, WhatsApp), your WhatsApp number if you give it and the time you consented, and your alert inbox with read status.
  • Device token: if you allow notifications, an identifier for your phone so we can send app notifications to it, and the app language.
  • Assistant usage: a daily count of the questions you ask Saathi (not the questions themselves).
  • Optional research data: how you use the app and referral attribution, only if you separately agree to research participation.
  • Support requests: what you send us when you ask for help.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your progress safe across devices.
  • To match new GeM bids to what your business supplies and send you alerts by app notification, email and — once you opt in and it is available — WhatsApp.
  • To answer your questions in Saathi using your own business details and matching bids.
  • To improve the app, only from research data you agreed to share.

We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Services that process data for us

  • Supabase — sign-in and our database, where your account and app data are stored with access limited to your own account.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivers app notifications to your device.
  • Resend — delivers email sign-in codes and bid-alert emails.
  • Google Gemini — generates Saathi’s answers. When you ask a question, we send it with your business profile answers, readiness, calculator results, alert settings and matching bids. We do not send your email, phone number or WhatsApp number. We do not store your questions or answers.
  • Meta WhatsApp Business Platform — only if you opt in to WhatsApp alerts, once that service is live: your number and the alert content.

Public GeM information

Bid details shown in Bid2Scale (bid numbers, buyers, items, dates, requirements) come from the public GeM bid portal. Always confirm details on gem.gov.in before you bid.

Your choices

  • Change keywords, states and alert channels any time in Bid alerts. Email and app notifications can each be turned off; WhatsApp starts off and needs your consent. Reply STOP to a WhatsApp alert to stop them.
  • Turn notifications off in your phone’s settings at any time.
  • Research participation is optional and can be withdrawn.
  • Delete your data or your account in the app (Profile → Delete my account) or on our account deletion page. Deleting your account removes your sign-in identity and all associated Bid2Scale data listed above.

Retention

We keep your account data while your account exists. Delivery records are removed after a few days, bid alerts for closed bids are removed shortly after the bid closes, and Saathi keeps only a daily question count. When you delete your account, your data is deleted promptly; limited records may be kept only where the law requires or to investigate abuse.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by authentication and row-level database rules, so each account can reach only its own data. Server keys for email, notifications and the assistant are held only on our servers. No online service can promise absolute security.

Children

Bid2Scale is meant for business owners and their authorised representatives, not for children.

Changes

If we make important changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact
