Privacy at GeM BizReady

GeM BizReady is an independent business-preparation project and is not affiliated with Government e-Marketplace (GeM). This policy describes the Android and web application.

Information we process

  • Account information: email address and, if Google sign-in is chosen, the basic identity information Google provides through Supabase authentication.
  • Business-planning information: calculator answers, readiness progress and reports the user chooses to save.
  • Optional research information: referral attribution and product-use events only after separate research consent.
  • Support information: a message and contact permission when the user requests workshop, CA or guidance follow-up.

The app does not ask users to give it a GeM password, Aadhaar number, PAN number, GST portal password or OTP.

Why and where information is used

Guest progress stays on the device. Signed-in progress is stored in Supabase so it can be restored across devices. Google processes sign-in data only when Google sign-in is selected. Consented research events are used to evaluate the project's educational tools. We do not sell personal information.

Control and deletion

Research consent is optional and can be withdrawn. A signed-in user can delete saved project data while keeping the sign-in identity, or permanently delete the account and associated project data from the app. See the account deletion instructions.

Retention and security

Account data is retained while the account exists or as needed to provide the requested service. Access is protected by Supabase authentication and row-level database policies. No internet service can promise absolute security.

Children and changes

The service is designed for business owners and authorised business representatives, not children. Material policy changes will be reflected on this page with a new effective date.
