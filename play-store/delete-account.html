Delete your account

GeM BizReady provides self-service account deletion in the Android app. This page is the account-deletion URL for the app on Google Play. Privacy policy.

Steps

  1. Open GeM BizReady and select Save progress.
  2. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
  3. Under Privacy controls, select Delete account permanently.
  4. Read the confirmation and approve deletion.
What is deleted

The Supabase authentication identity and its associated saved plans, research events, support requests and reported outcomes are deleted. Device-local guest information can be cleared separately through the app or browser storage. Limited records may be retained only where required by law or to resolve abuse/security incidents.

If you cannot sign in, email amaybanthia@gmail.com from the email address attached to the account and request deletion. We will confirm and complete the request within 30 days.
