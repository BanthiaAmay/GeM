Questions

Frequently asked

Is Bid2Scale part of GeM or the government?

No. Bid2Scale is an independent tool. It reads public bid information from the GeM portal and always links you back to the official page. Bidding itself happens on gem.gov.in.

Do I need to share my GeM password, PAN or Aadhaar?

No. Bid2Scale never asks for your GeM password, OTPs, PAN or Aadhaar numbers. Never share a GeM OTP with anyone.

How do bid alerts decide what to send me?

From your answers to “what does your business supply?”, Bid2Scale picks search words GeM uses in bid titles. A bid matches when it contains those words. You can change your choices any time under Bid alerts.

How often will I be notified?

At most one grouped app notification an hour and one email digest every 12 hours, and nothing between 10 pm and 7 am IST.

Which languages are supported?

English, Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu and Kannada.

How do I delete my account?

In the app open Profile → Delete my account, or use the account deletion page on this website.
