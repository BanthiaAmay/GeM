Account deletion

Delete your Bid2Scale account

You can delete your account and all its data yourself, in the app or right here. It takes about a minute.

In the app

  1. Open Bid2Scale and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Your sign-in and all Bid2Scale data linked to it are gone. You can uninstall the app; if you use it again you will start fresh.

Online deletion is not available on this page right now. Please delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete my account), or contact us below.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in identity (email account in Bid2Scale).
  • Your business profile answers, readiness checklist and calculator results.
  • Your bid alert settings, keywords, WhatsApp number and consent, alert inbox, and any queued notifications or emails.
  • Registered devices for app notifications and your Saathi usage count.
  • Optional research data and support requests.

Data stored only on your phone is cleared when you sign out, delete in the app, or uninstall. Limited records may be kept only where the law requires or to investigate abuse.

Can’t sign in?

Contact us through the developer contact on our Google Play listing from the email address on the account, and we will delete it for you.
